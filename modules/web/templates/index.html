Expression syntax & examples

x AND y requires both terms. x OR y matches either. x AND NOT y excludes y. x not in title excludes titles containing x.

Fields: in title, in abstract, in author, in category, or in all. Terms without a field search all fields. Use double quotes for phrases and parentheses to nest groups.

AND binds before OR. Main search terms use the selected field and are combined with the expression using AND. Start the expression with OR to use OR instead. A NOT condition needs a positive search term or category; for example, cat:cs.RO AND NOT survey.

Native arXiv fields work too: (cat:cs.RO OR cat:cs.AI) AND ti:robot. The preview shows the exact query sent to arXiv.
